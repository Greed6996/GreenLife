Ayurvedic Principles of Scalp Vitality

Hair restoration isn't just surface treatment—it is a holistic alignment of body composition, herbal nourishment, and stress relief. We leverage nature's three active botanical pillars to stimulate follicles natively.

Amalaki (Amla)

Rich in Vitamin C and potent antioxidants, Amla strengthens the hair shaft, limits premature pigment depletion, and triggers healthy cellular replication.

Nimba (Neem)

A supreme natural purifier. Neem neutralizes scalp congestion, prevents irritation-induced shedding, and balances the microbial ecology for strong growth.

Kesharaja (Bhringraj)

Known as the "King of Hair," Bhringraj increases blood circulation to active hair shafts, promoting the growth of thicker, deeper roots naturally.
