Personalized Nutrition

DNA Diet (Nutrigenomics)

Nutrigenomics is the study of how your individual genes interact with the foods you consume. Your genetic sequence dictates how you metabolize fats, absorb vitamins, process sugars, and clear toxins.

At Greenlife, we analyze key genetic markers (SNPs) to design a **DNA-personalized diet plan** customized specifically to your metabolic profile. This eliminates the guesswork of dieting and targets systemic inflammation and metabolic disorders at a cellular level.

  • ✔ Identify genetic sensitivities (gluten, lactose, sodium, caffeine)
  • ✔ Optimize macronutrient ratios based on genetics
  • ✔ Target micronutrient deficiencies (calcium, iron, vitamin D/B12 pathways)
